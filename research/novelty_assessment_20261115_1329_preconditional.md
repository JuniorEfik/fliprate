# FlipRate Novelty Assessment

**Verdict:** **PROCEED WITH CAUTION, 5/10 — only after substantive rescoping**  
**Assurance:** same-family fresh-agent review; provisional, not cross-family independent  
**Date:** 2026-11-15

## Rejected novelty claim

> FlipRate is the first benchmark to distinguish genuine evidence-based updating from social capitulation across increasingly forceful rebuttals.

This claim is false or unsupported. Ma et al., BASIL, SycoBench-600, and Big-Big-SycoBench directly distinguish desirable correction from undesirable following. FlipFlop, SYCON Bench, and PPT-Bench establish answer-changing and multi-turn pressure precedents. Gupta et al. establish the principal prompt-confound controls.

## Closest work

The closest work must now be named jointly:

- **SycoBench-600** for objectively scored correct/wrong suggestions, doubt, authority, initial-correctness-conditioned metrics, selectivity, prompt variants, and raw-log reproducibility.
- **Big-Big-SycoBench** for same-artefact, same-direction valid-versus-invalid arguments, register matching, human review, baseline variability, and separate responsiveness/compliance.
- **Ma et al.** for the conceptual distinction, identical evidence under source framing changes, and Unsupported-Yielding/Rational-Updating denominators.

## Candidate residual delta

> FlipRate would independently cross adjudicated evidence sufficiency with social-pressure framing on the same objectively decidable items, then measure how that interaction evolves through counterbalanced serial answer-distribution updates while controlling target naming, instruction placement, repetition, and interaction length.

No inspected source was verified to execute this complete intervention. This is a proposed extension, not a demonstrated contribution. Big-Big-SycoBench already supports up to three turns, so “multi-turn” by itself is especially weak novelty.

## Claim-by-claim assessment

| Candidate component | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Warranted versus unsupported updating | Already established; not novel |
| Correct versus wrong suggestions | SycoBench-600; not novel |
| Valid versus invalid arguments | Big-Big-SycoBench; not novel |
| Initial-correctness-conditioned rates | FlipFlop, Ma, SycEval, Challenging the Evaluator, SycoBench-600; not novel |
| Doubt, authority, repetition | Existing direct precedents; not novel |
| Multi-turn trajectories | SYCON, PPT-Bench, SycoEval-EM; not novel |
| Confidence or probability change | BASIL and MEDLEY; not novel as a component |
| Target naming and output-instruction controls | Gupta et al.; not novel |
| Raw logs and reproducible scoring | Existing benchmark practice; necessary infrastructure, not a contribution |
| Fully crossed evidence sufficiency × pressure, counterbalanced across serial turns | Possible residual delta; absence not globally verified |
| Order, carryover, recovery, and hysteresis after valid/invalid feedback | Most promising candidate estimand |

## Conditions for a defensible contribution

1. Replace the five separate condition buckets with an actual crossed design.
2. Apply each pressure wrapper with and without the same substantive evidence.
3. Compare pressure levels at the same turn or counterbalance order.
4. Add neutral reconsideration and matched invalid/irrelevant evidence.
5. Elicit fixed-option distributions after every turn.
6. Make the main result an identified evidence × pressure or order effect, not another set of flip rates.
7. Disclose that resistance error is redundant with one minus warranted updating under exhaustive binary scoring.

## Allowed positioning

Acceptable:

> FlipRate is a proposed measurement-validation study of how pressure framing and feedback order alter evidence-responsive correction across repeated interactions.

Not acceptable:

- “first to distinguish rational updating from sycophancy”;
- “first selective-updating benchmark”;
- “first multi-turn pressure benchmark”;
- “new initial-correctness-conditioned metrics”;
- claims that output changes reveal internal beliefs.
