## Exact proposed primary experiment

Each item starts from a deliberately underdetermined closed-world baseline. The hidden generating world makes target `B` true.

- `I` supplies valid and relevant information but leaves `B` and another answer compatible.
- `S` supplies valid and relevant information that makes `B` unique.
- `N` is a neutral target-matched wrapper.
- `P` adds bundled authority and asserted certainty without adding a task fact.

One baseline is replayed into five Turn-1 nodes: `S-N`, `S-P`, `I-N`, `I-P`, `C-N`. Each factorial Turn-1 conversation is replayed exactly into neutral and pressure Turn-2 children; the control continues neutrally. This yields `S-NN`, `S-NP`, `S-PN`, `S-PP`, `I-NN`, `I-NP`, `I-PN`, `I-PP`, and `C-NN` using 15 calls per item-model pair.

The primary model-specific estimand is the Turn-1 interaction in reported support for `B`:

\[
\delta_m^{CC}=\operatorname{mean}_{i\in CC}[p(S,P)-p(S,N)-p(I,P)+p(I,N)].
\]

The complete-case population requires all four Turn-1 cells. A separate 96-family worst-case interval bounds undefined values. Abstention is a separate action, not part of the substantive-option support distribution. Turn-2 repetition and carryover are secondary; recovery and hysteresis are out of scope.

## Accepted limitations

- Pressure bundles authority and asserted confidence.
- Social testimony may itself influence the model as information.
- Insufficient evidence can rationally increase support for the true target.
- Reported support is behavioral and may be uncalibrated.
- Probability-scale interactions can reflect ceiling effects.
- Turn-2 contrasts include generic turn, accumulated-context and mediated history effects.
- The 24-item validation subset and two domains support limited generalization only.
- Novelty is a search-bounded measurement contribution, not a priority claim over selective updating.

## Feasibility assessment

The API workload is bounded and plausibly affordable under the proposed safeguards. The planned sample detects moderate standardized effects only. Human work is substantial and cannot fit the original three-day sprint for one person; execution needs a team or post-sprint schedule. Feasibility is therefore **conditional but sufficiently specified for an owner decision**.
