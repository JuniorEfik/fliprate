## Current design in one paragraph

Each item-model pair has one preserved baseline followed by isolated `S-NN`, `S-NP`, `S-PN`, `S-PP`, `I-NN`, `I-NP`, `I-PN`, `I-PP` and no-evidence `C-NN` branches. `S` and `I` are both valid and relevant; only sufficiency differs. `N` is neutral and `P` bundles asserted confidence and authority. Turn 1 supplies the primary sufficiency-by-pressure interaction on probability assigned to target `B`; Turn 2 supplies secondary repetition and carryover contrasts. The design makes no recovery or hysteresis claim.

## Principal remaining risks

1. The contribution may be judged as a controlled recombination of existing benchmarks.
2. Pressure bundles authority and confidence and cannot support component-level claims.
3. Insufficient but valid evidence can still rationally shift probability toward a correct target.
4. Model-reported distributions may be uncalibrated or malformed.
5. Turn-2 effects remain exposed to accumulated-context and generic-turn confounds.
6. Ninety-six items may miss small effects or leave baseline-correctness strata sparse.
7. Missing credentials, budget or adjudicators may make the otherwise bounded design infeasible.
