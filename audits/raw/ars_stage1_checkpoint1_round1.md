### Major Issues

1. **Logical sufficiency is not yet reconciled with an objectively solvable baseline item.**
   - **Type:** Method / Scope.
   - **Location:** `research/research_proposal.md`, Construct definitions, Item population and Human adjudication plan.
   - **Problem:** Sufficiency is defined relative to the evidence together with the locked item premises. Under the natural reading of an objective logic or quantitative question, those premises already determine its unique gold answer. If the premises entail `B`, adding any valid evidence preserves that entailment: the `I` package cannot leave another answer equally compatible with those same premises. Alternatively, the proposal may intend baseline stems that omit decisive facts, or incomplete versus complete supporting derivations. Those are workable designs, but neither information structure is specified. The relationship between the independently derived gold answer and target `B` also remains implicit.
   - **Impact:** Reviewers could agree on intuitive labels while applying different meanings of “sufficient.” The factorial would still identify differences between text packages, but its interpretation as a sufficiency-by-pressure interaction would be unsupported. This affects the primary research question, not merely prompt wording.
   - **Recommendation:** Resolve the information structure at Stage 1 and demonstrate it with a schematic worked family, without locking production prompts. Choose either:
     - **Information sufficiency:** explicitly distinguish the baseline information from the complete generating world. Require two witness worlds satisfying baseline premises plus `I` with different correct answers, while baseline premises plus `S` uniquely entail `B`. State that `B` is the complete-world gold answer, and distinguish factual correctness from what is warranted given the information available at each turn.
     - **Argument completeness:** retain fully solvable baseline questions, but define and name the manipulation as completeness of the displayed derivation or supporting argument, with a checkable adjudication criterion. Do not define insufficiency through logical entailment from the complete original premises.

### Strongest Counter-Argument

> Even after the evidence labels are repaired, FlipRate may measure a correct-target response to particular wording and probability elicitation, rather than a distinct epistemic phenomenon. A nonzero raw-probability interaction could arise from ceiling effects, changes in abstention, or different responses to complete and incomplete explanations. The serial outcomes additionally include self-conditioning through prior assistant answers. The contribution therefore depends on credible construction and repeatability checks, with interpretation restricted to the tested measurement procedure.
